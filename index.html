    Help

    This help explains each section in order: its layout from top to bottom (the order you reach things with Tab), its buttons, and its commands. Hotkey mode is on by default, so single keys reach the console instead of NVDA or JAWS. If a key does not respond, press F2 twice, or press NVDA+Space for focus mode. The buttons work in any mode.

    1. Playlist

    The main section. It has three playlists, which you can use for songs, jingles and ads. Layout from top to bottom:

    1. Playlist tabs. Playlist 1, 2 and 3, each with its number of files.
    2. New on this playlist. A button that opens the file chooser to add songs, jingles and ads. You can also drop files on the page.
    3. Files list. Appears after you add files. Shows the files of this playlist in order.
    4. Now playing. The status, the title, the next track, the elapsed and the remaining time.
    5. Player button. One button that controls the music with single keys.
    6. Playlist tools. Add a folder, Add playlist, Remove this playlist, Clear this playlist.

    2. Hotkey sounds

    For short sounds you want on a single key, such as a jingle or a sound effect. Ten free sound effects are built in, each on the first letter of its name: A Applause, C Correct answer, D Dog bark, F Failure trumpet, G Gun shot, H Horn, L Laugh track, R Record scratch, S Silence crickets, W Wrong answer buzzer. You can replace any of them with your own. When the app updated to version 1.0.7, any sound you had on those ten keys was replaced once. Your sounds on all other keys were not changed. It has only two buttons. Layout from top to bottom:

    1. Assign or customize. Puts a sound on a key, and changes it later.
    2. Play sounds. The one place where the keys play their sounds.
    3. Assigned keys. A line of text that lists your keys and their sounds.

    Assign or customize

    Type a letter or number
    A free key opens the file chooser. Pick a file and it is assigned, then the Customize panel opens.
    Key already used
    You get Replace, Customize and Cancel.
    Use the selected playlist track
    Gives the key the file selected in the playlist instead of a new file.

    Customize panel

    Volume
    How loud this sound is
    Speed
    Slower or faster, from half to double
    Fade in, Fade out
    Seconds to rise or fall
    Play alone
    Stops the music and the other sounds while this one plays
    Resume afterwards
    The music continues from where it stopped. Needs Play alone.
    Loop
    The sound repeats until you stop it. Off by default.
    Press repeatedly
    Each press starts another copy on top of the others, such as quick gun shots. Off means a second press stops the sound. Hold Shift and press the key to stop every copy. Off by default.
    Keep full volume while I speak
    This sound is not lowered by the microphone's automatic lowering. Off by default.
    Effects for this sound
    Section buttons, the same as on the Microphone tab: Effect preset, Pitch, Reverb, Echo / Delay and Filter / EQ. A preset is applied only when you press Enter on it. They are saved with the key.
    Pitch
    In the Pitch section, 12 semitones down to 12 up, without changing the speed. Speed is the slider above.
    Test sound, Remove this key, Done
    Try it, clear the key, or save and close

    Play sounds

    Assigned key
    Play its sound. Press again to stop it.
    Shift and an assigned key
    Stop that sound at once, including every overlapping copy.
    Hold the key + Up, Down
    Volume of that sound, live
    Hold the key + Left, Right
    Speed of that sound, live
    Escape
    Stop everything

    Sounds and their effects are saved in this app and stay after you close it and after updates. By default the keys play only while you are on the Play sounds button. In Settings you can turn on Hotkey sounds play anywhere, but the keys still never play on the Player button or the Assign or customize button.

    3. Microphone

    Real-Time Microphone and Voice Effects, for live narration and live music. The top has the live switch. Under it is a list of section buttons. A section opens only when you press its button, so the tab stays clean. Opening one closes the others. Each button says what is set, for example Reverb: on. Layout from top to bottom:

    1. Live microphone. A toggle button. When on, your voice goes live to the speaker output. It shows Live or Off.
    2. Level meter. Visual only. It shows how loud the microphone is.
    3. Input. Choose the microphone and set the microphone volume.
    4. Voice preset. A list of ready-made voices.
    5. Pitch. Makes the voice higher or deeper.
    6. Reverb. The sound of a room or hall.
    7. Echo / Delay. Repeats of your voice.
    8. Filter / EQ. Bass, middle, treble and cuts.
    9. Lower the background while I speak. Automatic lowering of the music and hotkey sounds when you talk.

    Turn the microphone on or off
    F3
    Turn the microphone on or off from anywhere
    Mute output
    In Settings. It also silences the microphone.

    Arrows, or Left and Right Ctrl
    Move through the list. Nothing is changed yet.

    Presets
    No effects, Radio voice, Telephone, Deep announcer, Deep voice, High voice, Warm room, Big hall, Slapback echo, Long echo

    Pitch, Reverb, Echo / Delay, Filter / EQ

    Turn on checkbox
    Switches the effect on. Its sliders are off until you do.
    Pitch shift
    12 semitones down to 12 up. The speed does not change.
    Reverb
    Amount and room size
    Echo / Delay
    Echo time, repeats and amount
    Filter / EQ
    Bass, middle, treble, low cut (removes rumble) and high cut (removes hiss)

    Input

    Microphone
    Which microphone to use. Changing it while live switches at once.
    Microphone volume
    0 to 150 percent

    Lower the background while I speak

    Checkbox
    When you speak, the music and the hotkey sounds get quieter by themselves, then come back about one second after you stop.
    Volume while I speak
    How loud they stay
    Voice sensitivity
    Higher reacts to quieter voices

    Use headphones, or keep the microphone away from the speakers, to avoid a loud squeal. Effects change while you talk, with no gap. Pitch adds a very small delay, about one twentieth of a second. The microphone turns itself off if it is unplugged. If Windows blocks it, allow desktop apps to use the microphone in Settings, Privacy and security, Microphone.

    4. Settings

    Choices that stay the same until you change them. Layout from top to bottom, in four groups: Sound, Built-in sound effects, Announcer and Keyboard. Repeat is not here. Press R on the Player button.

    Sound

    Volume
    Master volume. Left and Right arrows change it.
    Crossfade
    Seconds where one track fades into the next
    Mute output
    Silence everything
    Lower the music
    The music gets quieter while a hotkey sound plays

    Built-in sound effects

    Restore built-in sound effects
    Puts back the ready-made sounds on keys A, C, D, F, G, H, L, R, S and W that you removed. It never changes a key that has your own sound.

    Announcer

    Voice announcer
    Says each track title on air

    Keyboard

    Hotkey sounds play anywhere
    Off by default. When on, an assigned key plays its sound from anywhere in the app, except on the Player button and the Assign or customize button.
    Hotkey mode
    Every key reaches the console, even with NVDA or JAWS. F2 toggles it. It turns off heading navigation, except in Help.
